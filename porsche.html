PROJECT 02

PORSCHE

UI Design | Web Design

INTRODUCTION

This concept explores how an automotive website can balance strong visual design with a clear, product-focused experience.

The focus was not only on creating a premium visual language, but on making the vehicle itself easy to discover, explore and configure. Every element was designed to support the product while keeping the experience intuitive and engaging.

VISUAL LANGUAGE

Product first. The car remains at the centre of the experience. Large imagery, clear model information and direct calls to action allow users to move naturally from browsing to exploring the vehicle.

THE EXPERIENCE

The interface uses a restrained visual system to keep attention on the vehicles while maintaining a clear hierarchy between discovery, information and action.
